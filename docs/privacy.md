# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the LaraDumps desktop app, including the version distributed through the Microsoft Store.

## Short version

- LaraDumps runs on your computer and works without an account.
- It does not collect, send or sell personal data. There is no telemetry, analytics or advertising.
- The dumps you send to the app stay on your computer.

## What the app processes

LaraDumps receives debug output (dumps, queries, logs, mail previews) that your own PHP applications send to it over a local connection on your machine. This data is shown in the app and kept on your computer only. It is never sent to us or to third parties.

The app stores its settings, such as window size, theme and project paths, locally on your computer.

## Network access

The app listens on a local port so your PHP applications can reach it. It does not send your data anywhere.

The Microsoft Store version does not check for updates on its own; updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store. Other versions may check GitHub for a newer release, which sends only a standard request to GitHub.

Links in the app, such as the documentation or GitHub pages, open in your web browser and are subject to the privacy policies of those sites.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any data, because the app does not collect any.

## Children

LaraDumps is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at [github.com/laradumps/app](https://github.com/laradumps/app/issues).
